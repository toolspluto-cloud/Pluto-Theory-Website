Pricing questions

Why isn't there a price on the page?

The automations are in early access, and we would rather show you a real demo and a clear scope than publish a number before the plan is finalised. Published prices will appear here as each automation becomes generally available.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Ad spend is always separate from the subscription, and paid directly to the ad platform.

Can I combine automations?

Yes. Bundles exist where the connection adds value, such as lead generation with CRM follow-up. Each automation also works on its own.

Is studio work part of the subscription?

Studio work is scoped and priced separately, so you always know what you are paying for.
