Questions people ask

Where do you shoot?

In Hyderabad. If your brand is somewhere else, our AI photoshoots create product and campaign images remotely, for brands anywhere in the world.

What kinds of photoshoots do you do?

Product photography, brand and campaign shoots, portraits and team photos, lifestyle shoots and event photography.

Do you plan the shoot or only take the photos?

We start from the concept, write the shot list with you, run the shoot and finish the edit. You are not left managing a shoot on your own.

What do we get after the shoot?

Edited, export-ready images for the places you will use them: website, e-commerce, ads and social.

Can you shoot video on the same day?

Yes, we also make brand films, reels and shorts. Mention it in your brief and we will plan stills and video together.

How much does a photoshoot in Hyderabad cost?

It depends on the number of products or looks, the location and how much editing you need. Send us a short brief and we will reply with a quote.
