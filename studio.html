Studio questions

Do I need an automation to work with the studio?

No. Studio work can stand on its own. It works best when it feeds an automation, like messaging that shapes your follow-ups or photos that fill your content queue.

How is studio work priced?

Each project has its own scope, fee and owner, quoted before we start.

Is human PR different from the PR workflow automation?

Yes. The automation finds opportunities and prepares pitches. The studio adds people who run the outreach and build relationships.

Tell us what you're making.

Brand, shoots, PR or a bespoke build. We'll reply with next steps and a clear scope.
