The job, defined

What you give it. What you get back.

You connect

  • Your website, or the agreed connection to it
  • Business context: services, products, locations
  • Target topics and the markets you serve
  • Search data access where you have it

You receive

  • A prioritised work queue, refreshed on a schedule
  • Page improvement recommendations
  • Content briefs ready for review
  • A plain-language progress report

You control

  • Which changes the automation may prepare or apply
  • What needs your approval before it goes live
  • The cadence of checks and reports

  • Recurring technical and on-page checks

Good to know

Honest limits

  • Rankings depend on Google, competitors and your market. No automation can guarantee a position.
  • We do not mass-produce pages without added value. Drafts are reviewed for accuracy.

Questions

SEO automation FAQs

Will this get us to the first page of Google?

No one can honestly promise that. The automation keeps useful SEO work happening every week and shows you what was done. We measure completed improvements alongside impressions, relevant clicks and enquiries.

Does it change my website on its own?

Only where you allow it and your site connection supports it. By default, changes are prepared for your review first.

What does a report include?

What was checked, what was improved, what is waiting for your review and how search visibility is moving, using the data you have connected.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
