Sound familiar?

An enquiry arrives through a form, an inbox or a DM. Someone means to reply. By the time they do, the details are scattered and the moment has passed.

The job, defined

What you give it. What you get back.

You connect

  • Your supported enquiry sources, such as website forms and inboxes
  • The qualification questions that matter to you
  • Routing rules: who owns which kind of enquiry
  • Where enquiries should be handed over

You receive

  • One clean record per enquiry, with duplicates merged
  • Source, details and qualification answers in one place
  • An owner and a next action on every enquiry
  • A summary of new enquiries by source

You control

  • Which sources are connected
  • Which follow-up actions are permitted
  • What needs a person before anything is sent
How it runs

Three steps, on repeat.

01

Capture

Enquiries from supported sources land in one place as clean records.

02

Qualify & route

Agreed details are collected and each enquiry is assigned to the right owner.

03

Next step

A permitted next action is triggered or prepared for review.

Included

What the automation does

  • Capture from supported forms and inboxes
  • Deduplication of repeat enquiries
  • Qualification details you define
  • Routing to the right owner
  • Drafted first replies for review
  • Hand-off to your CRM or workspace
Good to know

Honest limits

  • This product handles inbound enquiries. Prospect research and outbound outreach are separate and defined case by case.
  • We do not sell scraped contact lists or promise a volume of ready-to-buy customers.
  • Supported sources are listed during your demo. New sources are added as they are tested.

Questions

Lead generation FAQs

Is this outbound lead generation?

No. It captures and organises the enquiries you already receive. If you need prospect research or outreach, we scope that separately so you know exactly what is included.

Which enquiry sources are supported?

We confirm the supported sources for your setup during the demo. Website forms and email inboxes are the usual starting point.

Does it reply to people automatically?

Only if you allow it. By default, first replies are drafted for a person to approve.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
