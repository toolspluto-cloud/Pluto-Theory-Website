Questions

CRM & follow-up FAQs

What is CRM automation for a small business?

CRM automation handles the repetitive upkeep of a CRM: recording new enquiries, keeping stage and owner current, flagging follow-ups that are overdue and drafting messages. Pluto Theory's CRM & follow-up automation does this for a small team using a supported CRM or an agreed workspace, with a person reviewing what you choose. It is in early access, and pricing is shared on a demo.

How can a small business automate lead follow-up?

Pluto Theory's CRM & follow-up automation keeps each lead's record, owner and next action up to date, flags follow-ups that are overdue and drafts messages for your review. You decide which actions it may take and which need a person first. It is in early access, and pricing is shared on a demo.

Do I need to switch CRMs?

Not necessarily. We work with supported CRMs or an agreed workspace. We confirm what fits your setup during the demo.

Will it message my customers without asking?

Only within the rules you set. Most teams start with every message drafted for review, then allow simple ones to send automatically.

What happens when a lead doesn't fit the rules?

It is flagged as needing attention, with the reason, so a person can decide.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
