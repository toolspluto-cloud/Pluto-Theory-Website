The job, defined

What you give it. What you get back.

You connect

  • Your role, experience and point of view
  • Proof: results, stories and examples you are happy to share
  • Brand and tone preferences
  • Access needed for supported scheduling features

You receive

  • Relevant topic ideas based on your expertise
  • Post drafts in your voice, ready to review
  • An upcoming content calendar
  • Performance summaries where available

You control

  • Every post is approved by you
  • Topics you want covered or avoided
  • Which supported features are switched on

Good to know

Honest limits

  • LinkedIn restricts third-party automation of activity on its site. We do not automate browsing, mass connection requests or personal conversations.
  • Personal profiles and company pages have different supported features. We confirm which apply to you.
  • Drafts are a starting point. Your review keeps them accurate and yours.

Questions

LinkedIn content FAQs

Will it post as me without asking?

No. Every post goes through your review queue first.

Does it send connection requests or messages?

No. LinkedIn does not allow that kind of automation, and it is not part of this product.

How do the drafts sound like me?

They start from your own notes, stories and opinions, and get better as you edit and approve them.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
