Questions

PR workflow FAQs

Do you guarantee coverage?

No. Journalists decide what they cover. We make sure good opportunities are spotted and well prepared.

Who sends the pitches?

You, or our studio PR team if you add hands-on support. Nothing goes out without approval.

Is this the same as hiring a PR agency?

It is the process part of PR, automated. The relationship part is people work, which our studio can add.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
