Customers message you on WhatsApp because it is fast. Then the same five questions arrive all day, replies wait until someone is free, and the enquiry never makes it into your CRM.

The job, defined

What you give it. What you get back.

You connect

  • A WhatsApp Business Platform number (we help you set it up through Meta)
  • Your common questions and approved answers
  • The message templates you want for reminders and follow-ups
  • Where enquiries should go next: your CRM or our lead automation

You receive

  • Instant replies to common questions, in your tone
  • Enquiries captured with the details you need
  • Reminders and follow-ups sent at the right time
  • A clear hand-off to your team, with the conversation so far

You control

  • What the automation may answer, and what always goes to a person
  • Which reminders and follow-ups are sent, and when
  • Office hours and when a person takes over

Included

What the automation does

  • Replies to frequent questions from your approved answers
  • Enquiry capture into your lead or CRM process
  • Appointment, payment or order reminders using approved templates

  • Hand-off to a person, with the chat history

Good to know

Honest limits

  • Uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Business-initiated messages need the customer's opt-in and a template Meta has approved.
  • Meta charges for some messages. Those charges are separate from the subscription and shown before you start.
  • No bulk messages to people who haven't opted in, and no unofficial WhatsApp tools that put your number at risk.

Questions

WhatsApp automation FAQs

Do I need the WhatsApp Business app or the API?

This uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), which is what allows automated replies and reminders at scale. We help you set it up and confirm the right option for your number on the demo.

Can it send messages to all my customers at once?

Only to people who have opted in to hear from you, using templates Meta has approved. That protects your number and keeps messages welcome.

What happens when the automation can't answer?

The chat goes to a person on your team, with the conversation so far, so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

Are WhatsApp's own charges included?

No. Meta charges for some message types. Those charges are separate from the subscription, and we show you how they work before you start.

How much does it cost?

It is in early access. We share pricing on your demo, along with the included work, usage limits, setup and any extra charges.
