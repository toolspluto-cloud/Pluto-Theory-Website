Chatbot or AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent also does things: it can look up an order, update your CRM, book a slot in your calendar or raise a support ticket, using only the tools you allow. Most businesses need a bit of both, and we help you decide where answering ends and acting begins.

Trained on your information, not the whole internet

We connect the assistant to your own knowledge: products, prices, policies, FAQs and documents. It answers from that, says when it doesn't know, and passes the conversation to your team with everything they need to pick it up.

It sounds like your brand

An assistant is often the first conversation a customer has with you. Because Pluto Theory is also a branding studio, we write how it speaks: the tone, the greeting, how it says no, so it feels like your brand rather than a generic bot.

Safe by design

Every agent gets clear limits on what it may say and do, approval steps for anything that spends money or changes records, and a log of every conversation and action so you can review it. We tell you which AI services handle which data before launch.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent can also take actions with tools you allow it to use, such as looking up an order, updating your CRM, booking a meeting or raising a ticket. Many businesses need both: an assistant that answers, and acts when it is safe to.

Can it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp assistants run on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which needs a verified business account and an approved number. We help you set that up, and we explain Meta's messaging charges before launch.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and hands the conversation to your team with the context, instead of guessing. We set clear limits on what it may answer and do, and test it on tricky questions before it goes live.

Will customers know they are talking to AI?

We recommend telling them, and we design assistants to say they are AI and to offer a person whenever the customer asks.

How much does an AI chatbot or agent cost?

It depends on the channels, the systems it connects to and how many conversations it handles. We quote after a short call, and list running costs such as AI usage and messaging fees separately, so there are no surprises.
