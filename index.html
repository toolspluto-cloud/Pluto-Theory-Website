Questions

Before you subscribe

What is AI marketing automation for a small business?

It is software that runs a defined, recurring marketing job for you, such as SEO checks, lead follow-up, CRM updates or LinkedIn drafts, so the work no longer depends on someone remembering it. Pluto Theory is based in Hyderabad, India, and offers these automations on subscription to small businesses in India and worldwide. Every automation is in early access, and pricing is shared on a demo.

Is Pluto Theory an AI automation agency in India?

Pluto Theory is based in Hyderabad, India. About 70% of its work is AI marketing automations sold on subscription, and the other 30% is a studio team for brand, photography, PR and bespoke builds. Unlike a project-based agency, you subscribe to an automation that runs a recurring job. Every automation is in early access, and pricing is shared on a demo. For how to compare providers, read how to pick an AI automation agency in India.

What does “Marketing in a click” actually mean?

It describes the experience: choose an automation, connect what it needs, set your rules and activate it. It does not mean rankings, leads or sales appear instantly. It means the recurring work behind them stops depending on someone remembering it.

What is the 70/30 split?

About 70% of what Pluto Theory does is AI automation: subscription products that run defined, recurring marketing jobs. The other 30% is hands-on studio work, like brand, shoots, PR outreach and bespoke builds, for the parts that need people.

Will anything happen without my approval?

Only what you allow. You decide which actions run on their own and which wait for a review. Most teams start with everything waiting for approval.

Which accounts do I need to connect?

Only the ones the automation's job needs. Each product page lists its inputs, and we confirm the supported connections for your setup during the demo.

How much does it cost?

The automations are in early access. We share pricing for your chosen automation on the demo. Each plan will list its included work, usage limits and any extra charges. Advertising spend is never mixed into the subscription.

Is setup self-serve?

In early access, we set up each automation with you. If any part of activation needs our help, we tell you before you subscribe.

Which automations can I use now?

Every automation is currently in early access. Request a demo and we will show you what it does today, what it supports and what is still being built.

What if I need help?

Email info@plutotheory.com or use the support page. Studio work has its own named contact.
