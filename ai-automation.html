What AI automation is

Automation connects your tools so work moves by itself: a new lead lands in your CRM, gets a reply within a minute, and a follow-up task appears for the right person. AI handles the parts that used to need someone to read or write: understanding an email, pulling details out of an invoice, summarising a call, sorting enquiries by what the customer wants, drafting the first reply.

The result is fewer copy-pastes, faster replies and numbers you can trust, without hiring for work nobody enjoys.

Where it pays off first

The best first projects are frequent, repetitive and follow clear rules: tasks your team does many times a week in the same way. A five-minute job that happens fifty times a week is a strong candidate. A task that needs judgement every time is usually better helped by an AI assistant that drafts and a person who decides.

Built to be trusted

Every workflow we build keeps a record of what it did, puts a person in the loop wherever a mistake would be costly, and alerts you when something fails instead of failing silently. You own the workflows and the accounts they run on, with documentation your team can follow.

Questions people ask

What does an AI automation agency do?

It finds the repetitive work in your business and builds workflows that do it for you, using AI for the parts that need reading or writing: understanding a message, pulling details from a document, drafting a reply. Pluto Theory does this for businesses across India and worldwide, from Hyderabad.

What can be automated with AI?

Most frequent, rule-shaped work: lead follow-ups, CRM updates, data entry from invoices and forms, sorting emails and WhatsApp messages, scheduling, reports and first drafts. Work that needs judgement or a relationship stays with people, and we design the hand-over.

Do we have to change the tools we use?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already have, such as your CRM, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, spreadsheets, WhatsApp Business and accounting software. If a tool can't be connected, we suggest the simplest change.

Is our data safe?

We give each workflow only the data it needs, keep it in your own accounts where possible, and tell you which AI services will see which data before anything goes live. We design with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind, alongside your own policies.

How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on how many tools and steps a workflow has and how much AI it needs. We quote per workflow after a short call, and suggest starting with one so you can see it working before you automate more. Running costs, such as AI and software subscriptions, are listed separately.
