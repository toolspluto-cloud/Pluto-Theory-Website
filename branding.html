Branding that makes a company recognizable

Branding at Pluto Theory is how a company becomes recognizable. Not louder, clearer. We build the pieces people remember: the name, the look, the voice, and the rules that keep them consistent when the team grows and the channels multiply.

We start from what makes you different, then turn that into a system you can actually use. A brand that still feels like itself on a pitch deck, a product screen and a late-night reel. Identity boards that look pretty but break in the wild are not the goal.

When AI helps us explore directions faster, we use it. When taste and judgment decide the final line, people do. The result should feel creative and simple at the same time: a brand you can explain in one breath and still want to look at twice.

Brand and photography in one studio

Most brands meet their photographer after the identity is done, and the two never quite match. We do both. The same team that sets your colors, type and voice plans your photoshoot in Hyderabad or your AI photoshoot, so your images look like your brand from the first post.

Who it is for

Founders and teams who need a brand that feels intentional. Not generic, not overworked. New companies getting ready to launch, businesses that have outgrown their first logo, and products that need a character people remember. Our own mascots, Plu and Theo, are the proof that we take that last one seriously.

Questions people ask

What does Pluto Theory deliver in a branding project?

Depending on what you need: logo and visual identity, naming and taglines, brand voice and tone, brand guidelines, a social and digital kit, website look and feel, and brand characters or mascots. We scope it with you before we start.

Do you only work with businesses in Hyderabad?

We are based in Hyderabad and love meeting local teams in person, but branding work runs well over calls and shared boards, so we also work with founders and teams in other cities and countries.

Can you refresh our current brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Some brands need a quiet polish, some need a full rebuild when the old skin no longer fits. We look at what is working first and keep it.

Do you use AI for branding?

We use AI where it helps us explore directions faster. Taste and judgment decide the final line, and those come from people.

Can you photograph the brand once it is built?

Yes. That is one of the reasons to work with one studio. We run photoshoots in Hyderabad and AI photoshoots for brands anywhere, so the images match the identity from day one.

How much does branding cost?

It depends on scope. A refresh is a smaller project than a full identity with naming and guidelines. Tell us what you are building and we will reply with next steps and a clear quote.
