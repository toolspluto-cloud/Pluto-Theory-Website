What we are

01AI Automation

Workflows · Agents · Chatbots

Busy work, done by itself.

Every business has work that eats hours and teaches nobody anything: copying leads into a CRM, chasing follow-ups, typing invoices into a sheet, answering the same ten questions on WhatsApp. We find that work and hand it to AI.

Sometimes that is a workflow that connects your tools and runs in the background. Sometimes it is an AI agent or chatbot that talks to customers, qualifies leads or books meetings, and hands over to a person when it should. Either way it runs on the tools you already use, with a person in the loop wherever a mistake would matter.

Because we are also a creative studio, the parts your customers see, like replies, assistants and content, sound and look like your brand rather than a generic bot.

What you get

Lead follow-up

Enquiries captured, answered and followed up without anyone copying and pasting.

AI chatbots

Website, WhatsApp and Instagram assistants that answer from your own information.

AI agents

Agents that do multi-step jobs in your systems, with approval steps.

Document & data work

Invoices, forms and PDFs read by AI and entered where they belong.

Reports

Numbers gathered on a schedule, with a plain-language summary.

Team knowledge assistant

Your team asks questions of your documents and SOPs and gets sourced answers.

Content operations

One idea turned into posts, captions and newsletters in your brand voice.

Safe by design

Logs, limits and human checks, built in from the start.

Who it is for

Businesses in India and worldwide whose teams spend too much time on repeatable work. See AI automation → AI agents & chatbots →

02Branding

Identity · Voice · Systems

Brands with a point of view.

Branding at Pluto Theory is how a company becomes recognizable. Not louder, clearer. We build the pieces people remember: the name, the look, the voice, and the rules that keep them consistent when the team grows and the channels multiply.

We start from what makes you different, then turn that into a system you can actually use. Identity boards that look pretty but break in the wild are not the goal. A brand that still feels like itself on a pitch deck, a product screen, and a late-night reel. That is the goal.

When AI helps us explore directions faster, we use it. When taste and judgment decide the final line, people do. The result should feel creative and simple at the same time: a brand you can explain in one breath and still want to look at twice.

What you get

Logo & visual identity

Logo, type, color and layout rules that work from an app icon to a billboard.

Naming & taglines

Names, taglines and messaging that hold a point of view.

Brand voice & tone

How you sound in product, social, press and sales, with real lines to copy.

Brand guidelines

A living playbook your team can follow without guessing.

Social & digital kit

Post, story, reel and ad templates for Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube.

Website look & feel

Landing page and site design direction that turns visitors into conversations.

Brand characters

Mascots and illustration styles that give your brand a face people remember.

Refresh or rebuild

From a quiet polish to a full rebrand when the old skin no longer fits.

Who it is for

03Pluto Night

Community · Exchange · Culture

A room where ideas meet people.

Pluto Night is Pluto Theory’s community-building arm. It is where we create space for exchange: conversations, culture, and connection that outlast a single evening. The Pluto Theory team hosts and produces the night. The point is not a one-off party. The point is a recurring room people want to return to.

Brands can sponsor or partner when the fit is honest. Guests come for the people and the energy. We come to build something that feels alive: community as a craft, not as a hashtag bolted onto a campaign after the fact.

Details of formats and seasons will grow as Pluto Night does. What stays fixed is the intent. Gather well, exchange freely, and leave people with something they can carry into their work and their week.

How a Pluto Night works

01Hangout

Founders, creators and marketers talk shop like peers, not like an audience.

02Hot seat

One guest brings a real decision, bet or failure. The room pushes back. Friendly, but real.

03The game

A light game on top. Low stakes, quick rounds, and an easy way in for quieter guests.

04Community

People leave with new friends, sharper ideas and a reason to come back.

10 to 15 peers in the room · Hosted and produced by the Pluto Theory team

What you get

Community design

Who the room is for, why they should care, and how they find their way in.

Night production

The Pluto Theory team hosts and produces the night end to end, from invites to lights.

Exchange formats

Hot seats, games and open talk built for real connection, not empty networking.

Brand partnership

Sponsorship and collaboration when the brand truly belongs in the room.

Culture that travels

Moments, stories and content that live online long after the night ends.

A recurring ritual

A night people come back to, not a one-time splash.

Who it is for

Brands and builders who want community with substance, and partners who want to show up inside it the right way.

04Pluto Times

PR · Narrative · Reputation · Launch

Stories that land where they should.

Pluto Times is our PR desk, and PR at Pluto Theory is narrative work with a backbone. We help you say what matters, to the people who matter, in a way that feels true. Not like a press release wearing a costume.

That can mean shaping a founding story, preparing a launch, earning attention with media and creators, or protecting reputation when the stakes are high. We care about the angle as much as the outreach. A quiet story told well beats a loud story no one believes.

We work across classic media and modern creators, and we treat both with the same respect: clarity, timing, and a reason for someone to care. AI can help us research and draft faster. Judgment decides what ships.

05Tuto

Product development · In-house

We don’t just advise on products. We build them.

Tuto is Pluto Theory’s in-house product development program. It is how we initiate and build products under the same roof as the marketing and PR work. Not as a side note, and not as a slide about “product thinking.”

When an idea is strong, Tuto is where it becomes something people can use. That might be an MVP, a brand-led product, or an experiment we run to learn fast. The agency and the studio stay connected: brand, story, and product grow together instead of meeting for the first time at launch.

This is for partners who want makers in the room. Designers, builders, and strategists who will ship a first version, not only recommend one. Pluto Theory remains the house. Tuto is the workshop inside it.

How Tuto works

Step 01Seed.

Pluto Theory sparks the product idea or collaborates on it.

Step 02Build.

Associated developers, individuals or companies, build it. They hold equity in what they build, with no commercial terms.

Step 03Launch.

Pluto Theory markets it and brings seed and further investment. IP is held per product.

What you get

Product initiation

We start and own the build under Pluto Theory.

MVPs & first versions

Apps, dashboards and web products with focused scope and real users.

Experiments & prototypes

Small bets that answer big questions quickly.

Brand led product

Products that carry the same voice and visual system as the brand.

Cross team build

Strategy, design and production working as one loop.

Path to market

Packaging, story, marketing and launch support when the product is ready.

Who it is for

Founders and teams who want a product built with them, not a stack of recommendations left on the table.

06Production

Film · Photo · Design · Content

From concept to something you can ship.

Production is where ideas become assets. Film, photo, design, and content, planned, made, and finished so your brand can move. We care about craft and about deadlines. Beautiful work that never leaves the hard drive is not the job.

That covers campaign films and stills, social and reels, event capture, design systems in motion, and the everyday content that keeps a brand present. We start from the concept, stay through the shoot or the design pass, and hand over files ready to publish.

Production pairs cleanly with the other pillars: a brand system that needs assets, a Pluto Night that needs documentation, a Pluto Times launch that needs visuals, a Tuto product that needs a first story on screen. One house means fewer handoffs and a tighter final cut.
