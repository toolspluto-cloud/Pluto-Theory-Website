What an AI photoshoot is

An AI photoshoot creates product and campaign images with AI image tools instead of a physical set. We start from your real product photos and your brand, design the scenes, generate and select the best results, then refine and retouch them so they look like your brand and your product.

It works well for e-commerce images in many settings, seasonal and campaign sets, social content, and scenes that would cost too much to build for real. And because it is fully remote, it works for brands anywhere in the world.

Where a real shoot is still better

AI is a tool, not a replacement for every shoot. When you need exact likeness of real people, close-up detail that must be perfect, or the energy of a real event, a photoshoot in Hyderabad is the better choice. We will tell you honestly which one fits.

Art-directed by a branding studio

Anyone can type a prompt. What makes images useful is direction: knowing what your brand should look like, what your customers respond to and what will still look right next to your logo. That is our day job as a branding studio.

Questions people ask

What is an AI photoshoot?

Product and campaign images made with AI image tools instead of a physical shoot. We start from your real product photos and brand references, art-direct the scenes, then refine and retouch the results so they look like your brand.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Yes. AI photoshoots are fully remote, so we work with brands anywhere in the world. We are based in Hyderabad, India.

What do you need from us?

Clear photos of your product from a few angles, your brand guidelines or a few reference images you like, and where the images will be used. We take it from there.

Will the product look accurate?

We check every image against your real product photos and fix what does not match. For close-up, detail-critical shots, a real photoshoot in Hyderabad can still be the better choice, and we will tell you when it is.

Should AI images be labelled?

Some platforms and ad networks ask for AI-generated images to be disclosed. We tell you which images are AI-made so you can label them wherever the rules require it.

How much does an AI photoshoot cost?

It depends on the number of products, scenes and final images. Send us a short brief and we will reply with a quote.
