Brand identity for startups in India: what you actually need

By Pluto Theory · 1 November 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: a startup needs a clear name, a logo that works small, two or three colors, one or two typefaces, a voice and a simple one-page guide on day one. Full brand books, mascots and campaign systems can wait until you know what customers respond to. What matters most is that everything you ship looks and sounds like the same company.

The day-one brand kit

  • Name that is easy to say, spell and search, with the domain and social handles available
  • Logo in a full version and a small version (app icon, favicon, profile picture)
  • Colors: one main color, one accent, and neutrals, with exact codes
  • Typography: one typeface for headings, one for text (they can be the same)
  • Voice: three words for how you sound, plus a few real example lines
  • Imagery style: what your photos look like (light, color, people or no people)
  • A one-page guide so anyone on the team can make a post that looks right

What can wait

  • A 60-page brand book
  • Brand characters or mascots (unless they are central to the idea, as Plu and Theo are for us)
  • Motion systems and sound branding
  • Sub-brands for products you have not launched

Check the name before you fall in love with it

  1. Search the name on Google, Instagram and LinkedIn. Is it already a well-known brand in your space?
  2. Search the trade marks registry on the IP India website for similar names in your class of business.
  3. Check the domain and the main social handles.
  4. Say it out loud to five people and ask them to spell it. If three get it wrong, think again.

This is not legal advice. For a name you will build a company on, speak to a trademark professional.

Mistakes that make a brand look generic

  • Copying the category leader. If you look like the biggest brand in your space, people remember them, not you.
  • A logo that only works big. Most people will see it at 40 pixels on a phone.
  • Too many colors and fonts. Consistency beats variety.
  • Stock photos. They make every brand look the same. Your own photos, even simple ones, build recognition faster.
  • No voice. Words are half of a brand. If your website and your Instagram sound like different companies, customers notice.

Where photography fits

Photos are often the first thing people see, on your website, your listings and your feed. If your imagery does not match your identity, the brand falls apart in the places that matter most. That is why we plan brand photography and AI photoshoots as part of the identity, not after it.

When to rebrand

  • Your business has changed and the brand no longer explains it
  • You are moving upmarket and the look feels cheap next to new competitors
  • Customers keep confusing you with another company
  • Your team cannot use the brand consistently because there are no rules

Often a refresh is enough: keep what people recognize, fix what is broken.

Questions people ask

What does a startup need for its brand identity?

A clear name, a logo that works small, two or three colors, one or two typefaces, a defined voice, an imagery style and a one-page guide the team can follow.

How do I check if a brand name is available in India?

Search Google and social platforms, search the trade marks registry on the IP India website, and check the domain and social handles. For a name you will build on, ask a trademark professional.

When should a company rebrand?

When the business has changed, when the look no longer fits your market, when customers confuse you with others, or when the team cannot use the brand consistently.
