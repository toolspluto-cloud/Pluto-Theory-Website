AI automation

What does an AI automation agency do, and what does it cost in India?

By Pluto Theory · 15 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: an AI automation agency finds the repetitive work in your business (lead follow-ups, data entry, sorting messages, reports) and builds workflows that do it automatically, using AI for the steps that need reading or writing. In India, as of November 2026, published prices run from roughly ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 for one simple workflow, about ₹45,000 to ₹2 lakh for a workflow with an AI step, and ₹3 lakh to ₹10 lakh or more for a production AI agent, plus monthly software, AI usage and upkeep costs. Pick an agency that starts with one workflow, shows you its running costs line by line, and builds everything on accounts you own.

The price ranges in this guide come from Indian agencies' published price lists and from independent cost guides we read in November 2026. They change often, and every real quote depends on the work, so use them to sanity-check proposals, not as fixed rates.

What an AI automation agency actually builds

Most of the work falls into a few kinds of projects. The names vary between agencies, so it helps to know what sits underneath each one.

  • Rules-based workflows. Software moves data between your tools when something happens: a form is filled, a payment arrives, a row changes in a sheet. No AI is needed. This is the cheapest and most reliable kind of automation.
  • Workflows with an AI step. The same pipes, with a language model doing one job a person used to do: reading an email to work out what the customer wants, pulling the amount and GST number out of an invoice, summarising a call, drafting a reply for someone to approve.
  • Chatbots and assistants. A bot on your website or WhatsApp that answers from your own information and hands over to a person, or an internal assistant that answers your team's questions from your SOPs and documents. We cover these on our AI agents and chatbots page.
  • AI agents. Software that takes several steps on its own towards a goal, such as qualifying a lead, checking a calendar and booking a call. Agents are the most powerful option and the hardest to make dependable, which is why they cost the most.
  • Integrations. Connecting your CRM, accounting software, e-commerce store or ERP so the automations have clean data to work with. This is often the unglamorous half of the project.

A hypothetical example: a clinic could have every website and WhatsApp enquiry land in one sheet, get an instant reply with available slots, and create a follow-up task if the patient does not book within a day. The routing is a rules-based workflow; understanding a free-text message such as "can I come in Saturday for my knee" is the AI step.

And a quote that covers only the build tells you nothing about what the system will cost each month once it is live.

Running costs people forget to ask about

An automation keeps costing money after launch. A good proposal lists each of these separately:

  • Automation platform. Tools such as n8n, Make and Zapier charge monthly. As an example, n8n's cloud plans were listed at $24 a month for 2,500 workflow runs and $60 a month for 10,000 runs (less if billed yearly) when checked in November 2026 on CostBench's tracker of n8n's pricing. n8n's self-hosted Community Edition is free, but you then pay for and maintain a server.
  • AI model usage. You pay per use. Marketlube worked out that 10,000 call summaries a month could cost anywhere from about ₹430 to ₹43,200 depending on the model chosen (October 2026 list prices). The right model for each step is a design decision worth asking about.
  • Messaging fees. WhatsApp Business messages sent through Meta's platform are charged per message for most template types. Ask the agency to estimate this from your real message volumes.
  • Hosting and databases. Small, often a few thousand rupees a month for a modest tool, but it should be on the list.
  • Upkeep. Apps change their APIs, forms get new fields, prompts need tuning. Someone has to fix things when they break.

What changes the price

  • How many systems it touches. One sheet and one inbox is cheap. A CRM, accounting software, WhatsApp and an ad platform together are not.
  • Who sees the output. Internal tools can tolerate the odd rough edge. Anything a customer reads needs approvals, opt-in, tone checks and a clean hand-over to a person.
  • How much the AI decides alone. An AI that drafts for a person to approve is cheaper and safer than one that acts on its own across several steps.
  • The state of your data. Duplicates, free-text fields and three versions of the same customer list add cleaning work before anything can be automated.
  • Languages. Hindi, Telugu or mixed-language messages and voice need testing on your own real examples.
  • Failure handling. Logs, retries, alerts and an off switch add cost, and you want all of them.
  • Personal data. Workflows that handle customer or employee data need care under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and under your sector's rules.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. Which task would you automate first, and why? A good agency picks something frequent, repetitive and easy to measure, rather than proposing to automate everything at once.
  2. What will it cost each month once it is live? Ask for platform, AI usage, messaging, hosting and upkeep as separate lines, and how each grows with volume.
  3. Whose accounts will it run on? The workflows, AI keys and logins should sit in your accounts, so you can leave without rebuilding everything.
  4. Where does a person stay in the loop? Ask what happens when the AI is unsure, and which actions always need a human to approve.
  5. How will we know when it fails? You want alerts and a log of every run, not a silent stop you discover a week later.
  6. Which AI services will see our data, and where is it stored? Get this in writing before anything goes live.
  7. Can we see something similar you have built? A live demo, a recording or a diagram of the workflow tells you more than a slide.
  8. What is not included? Data cleaning, extra integrations, changes after launch and training your team are common extras.
  9. How will we measure whether it worked? Agree a simple before-and-after number, such as hours spent per week or time to first reply.

Red flags

  • A promise to automate your whole business, or to replace your team.
  • Running costs that are vague, bundled into one number, or missing.
  • Everything built on the agency's own accounts, with no plan to hand it over.
  • No questions about your current process before a quote arrives.
  • "Live in a week" for anything customer-facing that touches several systems.
  • No answer to what happens when the AI gets something wrong.
  • No documentation, so only the agency understands how it works.

Agency, freelancer or doing it yourself?

If you need two apps to talk to each other, such as a form that adds a row to a sheet and sends you an email, you can usually set it up yourself on Zapier, Make or n8n in an afternoon. A skilled freelancer suits a few well-defined workflows with a clear owner on your side. An agency or studio makes more sense when the work spans several systems, involves customers or personal data, or needs someone accountable for keeping it running. Whoever you choose, insist on the same basics: your accounts, written documentation and a clear monthly cost.

If you are comparing several providers, send each the same short brief, the way we suggest for creative work in how to choose a branding agency: the task, how often it happens, how long it takes today, the tools involved and who checks the result.

Questions people ask

What does an AI automation agency do?

It finds repetitive work in your business, such as lead follow-ups, data entry, sorting messages and reports, and builds workflows that do it automatically across the tools you already use. AI handles the steps that need reading or writing, and a person stays in the loop where a mistake would be costly.

How much does AI automation cost in India?

Prices published by Indian agencies and cost guides, checked in November 2026, run from roughly ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 for a single simple workflow, about ₹45,000 to ₹2 lakh for a workflow with an AI step, and ₹3 lakh to ₹10 lakh or more for production AI agents. Monthly upkeep retainers are commonly ₹20,000 to ₹1.5 lakh, plus software and AI usage costs.

How long does an AI automation project take?

Indian cost guides checked in November 2026 put a single workflow at about one to three weeks, a WhatsApp or website chatbot at two to four weeks, and an AI agent or multi-channel system at roughly four to twelve weeks, depending on how many systems it connects.

Can I automate with Zapier or n8n myself instead of hiring an agency?

Yes, for simple connections between two or three apps. An agency earns its fee when a workflow touches several systems, uses AI on customer-facing messages, handles personal data, or needs error handling and monitoring so it does not fail silently.
