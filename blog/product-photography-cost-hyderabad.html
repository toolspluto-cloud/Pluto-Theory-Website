How much does product photography cost in Hyderabad?

By Pluto Theory · 1 November 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer: in 2026, Hyderabad studios and marketplaces list simple e-commerce product photos from roughly ₹350 to ₹1,500 per image, and shoot days from around ₹10,000 to ₹25,000. Styled campaign shoots with models, stylists and a location cost much more, often ₹50,000 to ₹1.5 lakh or more per day. What you pay depends on how many products, how many angles, the style and how much editing you need.

Those numbers come from public price lists of Hyderabad studios and booking platforms that we checked in October and November 2026. Prices change, and every quote depends on the brief, so treat them as a starting point, not a promise.

What changes the price

  • Number of products and angles. Ten products from three angles is 30 final images, not ten.
  • Background and style. A plain white background is the cheapest. Built sets, props and colored backdrops take longer to light and style.
  • People. Models, a stylist and a make-up artist each add a day rate. Hand models cost less than full models.
  • Location. Shooting in a studio is usually cheaper than moving a crew and lights to a café, a factory or an outdoor spot.
  • Editing. Basic color correction is often included. Cut-outs (PNG files), shadows, color variants and heavy retouching are often extra.
  • Where the photos will be used. Marketplace listings, a website, social media or paid ads. Some photographers price ad and billboard use higher.
  • Turnaround. Rush delivery usually costs more.

Per image or per day: which is better?

Per image is easier to budget when you have many similar products on a white background. Per day is better when each shot needs styling, when you want lifestyle images, or when you are not sure how many final images you need. For a mix, ask for a day rate with an estimate of how many finished images the day will realistically produce.

Costs people forget to ask about

  • PNG cut-outs or transparent backgrounds
  • Extra rounds of retouching
  • Props, flowers, food styling or set materials
  • Couriering products to the studio and back
  • Raw files (many photographers do not include them)
  • Reshoots if the product changes or arrives damaged
  • GST on the final invoice

How to get an accurate quote

Send every photographer the same short brief, so the quotes are comparable:

  1. What the products are, how many, and their size
  2. How many angles or images you need per product
  3. Where the photos will be used (marketplace, website, social, ads)
  4. Two or three reference images of the style you like
  5. Whether you need models, hands, a location or video
  6. Your deadline and your budget range

A good photographer will ask questions back. A quote that arrives in two minutes without any questions is often a template.

When an AI photoshoot is the better deal

If you need many variations of the same product (different backgrounds, seasons or settings), or your team is not in Hyderabad, an AI photoshoot can cost less than building real sets. Questions people ask

How much does product photography cost per image in Hyderabad?

Public price lists from Hyderabad studios and marketplaces in 2026 show roughly ₹350 to ₹1,500 per image for simple white-background e-commerce photos. Styled and lifestyle shots cost more.

Is a day rate or per-image pricing better?

Per image suits many similar products on a white background. A day rate suits styled, lifestyle or model shoots where each set-up takes time.

What should I send a photographer to get a quote?

The products and how many, images per product, where the photos will be used, reference images, whether you need models or a location, your deadline and budget range.
