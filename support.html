Support questions

How do I pause an automation?

Tell us and we will pause it. As the workspace grows, pausing will be a single switch on each automation.

Can I change what needs my approval?

Yes, any time. Tell us what you would like to change and we will update the rules with you.

Who do I contact about studio work?

Use the contact form and choose Studio, or email us. Studio projects have a named owner.

Where is my data used?

Only for the job the automation does for you. See our privacy policy for how this website handles information.
