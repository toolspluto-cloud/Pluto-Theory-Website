Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Pluto Theory, a branding and photography studio based in Hyderabad, India. You can reach us at plutotheory.pt@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form. When you send the form we receive your name, email, company or project, the service you picked and your message. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms (web3forms.com).
  • Emails you send us. We keep them so we can reply and follow up.
  • Visit statistics. The site can use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use it, such as which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked. When they are switched on, these tools may set cookies and collect information about your device and visit.
  • Small preferences in your browser. Things like whether sound is on and the idea you typed into “Pitch it to Plu and Theo” are stored in your own browser so the site remembers them. They are not sent to us.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard technical logs to run and protect the service.

How we use it

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry and work with you. We use visit statistics to improve the website. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to talk to you and, if we work together, for our records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you have sent us by emailing plutotheory.pt@gmail.com. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
